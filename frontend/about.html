Mission

Why we built this

Egypt's padel scene had no central home. Players competed in scattered club tournaments with no unified rankings, no tracked history, no way to prove your level. APadel fixes that.

We run official ranked tournaments, maintain transparent player standings, and give every win a permanent record. Every match played on this platform counts.

How it works

01

Register your account

Create a player profile, set your details, and join Egypt's competitive padel network in under two minutes.

02

Enter a tournament

Browse open events, register your doubles team, pay the entry fee, and await organiser confirmation.

03

Compete and climb

Every win earns ranking points. Finals and titles unlock bonus points. Your record is permanent.
